Teams

  • Scuderia Ferrari, the most iconic team in Formula 1 history, has competed in every season since the championship began in 1950.
  • Ferrari is known for its passionate fan base (the Tifosi) and its legendary red cars that have won 16 Constructors' Championships.
  • Notable achievements: 16 Constructors' Championships, 15 Drivers' Championships, and over 240 race wins
