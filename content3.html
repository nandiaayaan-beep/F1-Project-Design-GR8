Drivers

  • Lewis Hamilton is one of the most successful drivers in Formula 1 history, combining elite race pace with consistency, tire management, and exceptional qualifying speed.
  • His career has stretched across McLaren, Mercedes, and Ferrari, showing his ability to adapt to different teams, rule eras, and championship battles.
  • His records include 7 World Championships, 105 Grand Prix wins, 104 pole positions, and more than 200 podium finishes.

Lewis
Hamilton

  • Ferrari — 7-time World Champion
  • 2007–2012 (McLaren), 2013–2024 (Mercedes), 2025–Present (Ferrari)

  • Max Verstappen is known for fearless racecraft, sharp car control, and the ability to attack at the limit from the opening lap.
  • He became F1's youngest race winner with Red Bull in 2016 and later built one of the most dominant title runs of the modern era.
  • His records include 4 World Championships, 71 Grand Prix wins, and the single-season record of 19 wins in 2023.

Max
Verstappen

  • Red Bull Racing — "Mad Max"
  • 2015–2016 (Toro Rosso), 2016–Present (Red Bull Racing)
🏆 4
  • Ayrton Senna is remembered for extraordinary qualifying speed, wet-weather brilliance, and one of the most intense competitive mindsets in F1 history.
  • His rivalry with Alain Prost shaped the late 1980s and early 1990s, while his six Monaco Grand Prix wins helped define his legend.
  • His legacy includes 3 World Championships, 41 Grand Prix wins, and 65 pole positions.

Ayrton
Senna

  • McLaren — "The King of Monaco"

🏆 3
  • Michael Schumacher became the symbol of Ferrari's golden era, combining speed with intense preparation, fitness, and technical feedback.
  • His partnership with Ferrari helped turn the team into the dominant force of the early 2000s.
  • His achievements include 7 World Championships, 91 Grand Prix wins, 68 pole positions, and 5 consecutive titles from 2000 to 2004.

Michael
Schumacher

  • Ferrari — "The Red Baron"
  • 1991–1995 (Benetton), 1996–2006 (Ferrari), 2010–2012 (Mercedes)
🏆 7
  • Juan Manuel Fangio, known as "El Maestro", dominated the dangerous early years of the Formula 1 World Championship.
  • He was famous for smooth car control, mechanical sympathy, race intelligence, and the ability to win with several different teams.
  • His achievements include 5 World Championships, 24 Grand Prix wins, and titles with Alfa Romeo, Mercedes, Ferrari, and Maserati.

Teams

  • Scuderia Ferrari is the only team to have competed in every Formula 1 season since the World Championship began in 1950.
  • Ferrari is known for its red cars, Maranello heritage, passionate Tifosi fanbase, and some of the sport's most famous champions.
  • Notable achievements: 16 Constructors' Championships, 15 Drivers' Championships, and more than 240 Grand Prix wins.

Ferrari — "The Prancing Horse"

1950–Present (F1)

🏆 16
  • McLaren entered Formula 1 in 1966 and became one of the sport's most successful and innovative teams.
  • The team is known for its papaya livery, Woking base, engineering culture, and legendary drivers including Emerson Fittipaldi, Ayrton Senna, Mika Häkkinen, and Lewis Hamilton.
  • Notable achievements: 10 Constructors' Championships and more than 200 Grand Prix wins.
