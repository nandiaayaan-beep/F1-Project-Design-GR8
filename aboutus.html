About This Project

Background

This website is a solo digital design project created by Aayaan Nandi. It introduces Formula 1 through the sport itself, the engineering behind the cars, the current drivers and teams, and some of the most important names in F1 history. The goal is to make Formula 1 easier to understand for new fans while still giving enough detail for people who already enjoy the sport. Feedback is welcome because strong design is built through testing, revision, and listening to real users.

Viewing Options

Ways to watch Formula 1:


  • Budget: A regional sports broadcaster or local streaming partner can be the cheapest way to watch races, especially if you only need the main Grand Prix coverage. The tradeoff is that features, replay access, camera angles, and stream quality may vary by country and provider.
  • Best Value: F1 TV Pro is often the strongest option for dedicated fans where it is available. It usually includes live sessions, onboard cameras, timing data, team radio, replays, and support-series coverage such as F2, F3, and F1 Academy.
  • Premium: A full-featured package is best for viewers who want the highest-quality stream, multiple devices, replays, extra camera feeds, and the most complete race-weekend experience.

The best choice depends on where you live, your budget, and how much detail you want while watching. Casual viewers may only need a basic broadcast. Fans who follow every practice, qualifying, sprint, and race will get more value from a dedicated F1 package. Because availability, features, and prices change by region, the official F1 TV page is the best place to confirm the current options before subscribing.
