Background

This website is a solo development project created by Aayan Nandi. The website is designed to showcase information about Formula 1, including the sport, cars, drivers, and teams. The website is part of a digital design summative, in which a 5 page website is required to be created. The purpose behind the creation of the website is to inform the users about the sport of Formula 1, aiming to earn their interest in the sport, and bring audiences toward watching the sport. Please ensure to leave feedback and future improvements which can be integrated into the website in the right column. My goal is to ensure your suggestions come first, in order to make my websites and future projects as appealing as possible to users in the future.

Best Plan

Best plans for you:


  • Budget: For the best budget friendly viewership of F1, Fancode can reach about 6.55USD per a full season, or 0.55USD per race weekend. However, this often features more technical difficulties when watching F1, ad interruptions, crashes, lower streaming quality, as well as not having access to all the features that an F1 subscription would give you especially in terms of viewing the rankings of drivers and teams as well as multiple camera angles when watching F1.
  • Best Value: F1 TV Pro is probably the best value for live coverage, being about USD30 a year. It features all F2 and F3 sessions, alongside live races with many more camera angles and user friendly features, as well as no ads or interruptions due to it being on the F1 website.
  • Premium: The premium subscription features everything at about 40 USD a year, featuring 4k live streaming of the races, multi-device, as well as many more multi-view options. It also covers e-sports as well as additional features and F1 academy. It features everything available.

If budget is a serious concern, Fancode is the best option. If the budget isn’t too large of a concern, F1 TV Pro is most probably the best value. If budget isn’t a problem altogether, opt for the premium subscription. Remember, only opt for the plan based on the features. For example, if you don't mind lower resolution streaming and/or don't need multiple devices, pro works just fine. If you have multiple devices, and need a higher resolution streaming experience, premium is the way to go.
