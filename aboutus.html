Background

This website is a solo development project created by Aayan Nandi. The website is designed to showcase information about Formula 1, including the sport, cars, drivers, and teams. The website is part of a digital design summative, in which a 5 page website is required to be created. The purpose behind the creation of the website is to inform the users about the sport of Formula 1, aiming to earn their interest in the sport, and bring audiences toward watching the sport. Please ensure to leave feedback and future improvements which can be integrated into the website in the right column. My goal is to ensure your suggestions come first, in order to make my websites and future projects as appealing as possible to users in the future.
